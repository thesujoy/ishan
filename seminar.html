The Last Seminar I Attended

Recently, I had the opportunity to attend a seminar that brought together thoughtful ideas, interesting conversations and several different perspectives.

What stayed with me most was not simply listening to the speakers, but noticing how one idea could naturally lead to another. Some of those thoughts continued long after the seminar itself had ended.

Experiences like this are a pleasant reminder that stepping outside everyday routines, listening carefully and remaining curious can sometimes lead to unexpected ideas.
